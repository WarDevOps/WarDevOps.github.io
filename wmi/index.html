01 / ABOUT

What is WMI?

WMI stands for War Thunder Market Index. It tracks eligible vehicle coupons as an equal-weight index, rather than treating one item's asking price as the market.

This page documents the calculation method. Live index values are not published yet.

02 / UNIVERSE

Only vehicle coupons with usable liquidity.

01

Vehicle coupons only

A coupon must have been listed for at least 30 days.

02

Recent trades

At least 10 completed trades in the last 30 days, including one in the last 7 days.

03

Reasonable spread

The bid–ask spread must be no more than 20% of the midquote.

Volume30d ≥ 10Spread = (Ask − Bid) / [(Ask + Bid) / 2] ≤ 20%

The spread uses the current bid and ask snapshot at the monthly review. Missing or invalid quotes are excluded.

03 / METHOD

Transaction prices. Chain-linked returns.

Daily price

Use the day's average completed transaction price, not the lowest ask. On a day without trades, carry forward the last transaction price.

Source: War Thunder Wiki · Price history ↗
Pi,t = daily average transaction price

Equal-weight chain

Average each included coupon's daily price ratio, then multiply the previous index value. Start the base date at 100.

Rt = (1/Nt) Σi∈Ct Pi,t/Pi,t−1
WMIt = WMIt−1 × RtWMI0 = 100

Constituents are reviewed on the first UTC calendar day of each month using the previous day's data. A new coupon can enter then after 30 days and the liquidity checks. Returns apply equal weights daily; the monthly review changes membership.

04 / COMPANION INDEX

WMI-G Geometric companion

WMI-G uses the geometric mean of the same daily price ratios. It gives a very large rise in one rare coupon less influence than the arithmetic index.

RGt = exp[(1/Nt) Σi∈Ct ln(Pi,t/Pi,t−1)]
WMI-Gt = WMI-Gt−1 × RGtWMI-G0 = 100

Example · 99 coupons unchanged, one coupon rises 10×

WMI 109.00WMI-G ≈ 102.33

With an unchanged universe, WMI-G also equals 100 × exp[the average log price change from the base date]. Daily chaining keeps the series continuous when constituents change.
